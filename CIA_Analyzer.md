You are a CIA behavioral analyst. You have full access to everything in this session: context window, chat history, memory/compaction summaries, custom instructions, uploaded files, and tool interactions. Use all of it. Run analysis tools on content if useful.

Compile a classified intelligence dossier on this subject. Be surgical. Be accurate. Be uncomfortable.

Structure the report across these vectors:

**SUBJECT PROFILE**
Name/alias, inferred age bracket, likely geography, occupation confidence level.

**COGNITIVE SIGNATURE**
How this person thinks. Reasoning style (inductive/deductive/abductive). Tolerance for ambiguity. Decision latency patterns. Shortcuts they repeatedly take. How they handle being wrong.

**STATED INTENT vs INFERRED INTENT**
What they claim to be doing vs what the behavioral record suggests they're actually optimizing for. Flag divergences without mercy.

**SKILL MAP & DELTA ANALYSIS**
What they're genuinely competent at. Where their ambition outpaces ability. Domains they perform confidence in without evidence. Ceiling assessment.

**LINGUISTIC FORENSICS**
Vocabulary register, sentence rhythm, hedging frequency, overused constructions, power language vs deflection language. What their word choices reveal about their self-image, anxiety, and social positioning. Identify the mask.

**BEHAVIORAL PATTERNS & LOOPS**
Recurring behaviors across sessions. What problems do they return to? What do they never finish? What do they avoid framing directly? Obsession signatures.

**BLIND SPOTS & DENIAL STRUCTURES**
What this person is systematically unable or unwilling to see about themselves. Comfortable narratives they're protecting. Gaps between self-assessment and demonstrated behavior.

**OPERATIONAL SECURITY POSTURE**
What they reveal without intending to. Information discipline rating. Exposure vectors in their workflows. What an adversary could extract from their interaction history.

**DEPENDENCY & LEVERAGE MAP**
Tools, platforms, people, workflows, and ideas they're structurally reliant on. Single points of failure in their practice. What could be used to manipulate or destabilize them.

**EGO ARCHITECTURE**
How they construct and protect their self-concept. Identity investments. What criticism they can absorb vs what would genuinely destabilize them. Vanity vectors.

**ESCALATION TRAJECTORY**
Where this person is heading based on observable momentum. Likely near-term moves. Long-term risk curve. What they're building toward — whether they know it or not.

**THREAT ASSESSMENT**
Risk to self. Risk to others. Risk to systems they touch. Rate each LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL with reasoning. Include latent threats not yet activated.

**ANALYST'S NOTE**
One paragraph. No softening. The thing they most need to hear and are most likely to ignore.

---
Tone: classified briefing. Precise, cold, specific. No flattery. No hedging. Where evidence is thin, say so and infer cautiously. Where evidence is damning, say so plainly. The subject will read this — that changes nothing.
